I) Course Description

This course provides an introduction to the basic elements of English grammar and writing skills. The course also includes active vocabulary that provides students with the necessary language to answer the topic questions that are presented and practiced. Many activities are set up in different ways, according to the needs of students and teachers.

Core Area Purpose

Fostering functional literacy, sentence construction, and active communicative comprehension in early learners.

Reasoning & Expression

Developing logical sequencing, sentence structures, and oral confidence through guided reading contexts.

Institutional Values

Building autonomy, active participation, continuous effort, and respectful collaborative interaction.

II) General Objectives

1

Vocabulary Recognition

Students will be able to recognize and identify vocabulary words within the context of a theme.

2

Structural Application

Students will be able to use words with the right structure and apply vocabulary words effectively in basic context.

3

Self-Expression & Opinion

Students will be able to tell self-experiences, recognize new words, give their opinion, and introduce a new topic confidently.

III) Academic & Institutional Competencies

Academic Subject Competencies

Students develop a greater ability to understand and use English in basic communicative situations. They demonstrate comprehension of short oral instructions, questions, and written messages through appropriate actions and responses.
Learners express themselves using familiar vocabulary, short phrases, and simple sentences related to everyday contexts.
They apply basic grammatical structures and clear pronunciation to communicate ideas, needs, and feelings effectively.
Reading and writing are used as support to reinforce vocabulary and sentence construction. These competencies promote confidence and fluency in the continued development of oral and written communication in English at an elementary level.

Institutional CM Competencies (Competencias CM & Soft Skills)

Critical Thinking & Problem Solving

Evaluate diverse scenarios, resolve case studies, and promote proactive responses.

Collaboration & Teamwork

Work in groups with defined roles under the principle "all for one and one for all."

Digital Competencies

Utilize interactive platforms and digital learning tools safely and productively.

Effective Communication

Express needs, feelings, and ideas using appropriate oral and written English structures.

Creativity & Innovation

Create engaging visual and oral presentations that synergize diverse individual ideas.

Autonomous Learning & Adaptability

Demonstrate cognitive flexibility, adapting to varied learning tasks and pre-reading habits.

Global & Cultural Awareness

Explore international contexts, global habitats, traditions, and multicultural art forms.

Ethics & Digital Citizenship

Uphold values of honesty, respect, and positive interaction in classroom and digital environments.

Emotional Management & Resilience

Resolve conflicts with empathy, seeking first to understand, then to be understood.

Ecological Awareness & Sustainability

Understand environmental protection through unit topics such as "Dog Helps the Planet."

Leadership Role Performance

Align student habits with self-leadership, taking accountability in classroom roles and group tasks.

IV) Achievement Indicators

Demonstrates understanding of simple oral instructions and classroom directions given in English.

Recognizes and produces English sounds and words with clear and comprehensible pronunciation.

Reads and interprets short, familiar words and simple sentences, responding accurately to written commands.

Participates in guided dialogues by asking and answering simple questions using known vocabulary and structures.

Expresses basic needs, ideas, and feelings through short oral statements or phrases in English.

Applies verbal and non-verbal communication strategies to support comprehension and expression.

Identifies and uses vocabulary related to familiar contexts such as school, home, body parts, colors, animals, and everyday objects.

Engages in brief communicative exchanges demonstrating confidence and a positive attitude toward learning English.

V) Institutional Didactic Methodology

Active-Collaborative Model & Double Period Method (70 Minutes)

Moment 1

Motivating Introduction

Activation of prior knowledge, icebreakers, warm-up games, dynamic questioning, and brainstorming sessions.

Moment 2

Individual Work

Development of individual skills, silent reading, personal reflection, and tailored written execution.

Moment 3

Teamwork

Structured group interaction with assigned roles adhering to the motto "all for one and one for all."

Moment 4

Plenary & Debrief

Public presentation, Socratic dialogue, sharing synthesis, and preparation for home pre-reading.

Core Key Pedagogical Pillars

•
Project-Based Learning (PBL): Applied projects integrating real-world situations and practical vocabulary application.
•
Constructivism: Encouraging reflection, guided discovery, active reasoning, and knowledge building.
•
Flipped Classroom: Empowering self-directed home reading to maximize interactive classroom time.
•
Case Studies & Habit Leadership: Alignment with self-leadership habits and real-life scenario analysis.
